A systems-level exploration of how AI agent networks can coordinate distributed scientific gatherings — venues, travel, vendors, and schedules — while preserving human oversightA person reviews and approves the plan before anything executes. The agents propose; they never decide on their own. at every decision point.

The point: each agent owns a constrained domain, and coordination runs through a shared knowledge graph — so the work between domains finally has an owner.
